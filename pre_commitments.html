Wheel basket (PRE_COMMITMENT_WHEEL.md)

Basket selection (locked)

  • Primary 9-name basket: AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL, JNJ, KO, PG, WMT, JPM, PEP
  • Robustness 7-name strict: drops JPM, PEP
  • Robustness 8-name: adds JPM only

5-layer wheel architecture

  • Layer 1 Regime-conditional delta: VIX bottom Q → 0.15, mid → 0.20, top → 0.25
  • Layer 2 Per-name vol-adjusted strikes: RV>35% widens delta by +0.05
  • Layer 3 Per-name 30-day RV forecaster: XGBoost on HAR-RV + exogenous, sizing = clip(target_rv/forecast, 0.4, 1.5), skip if forecast > 95th pctile
  • Layer 4 Earnings-aware management: 7-day blackout pre-earnings, narrow CCs
  • Layer 5 Fundamental-only defensive rolls: entry premise broken (EPS guidance cut >20%, dividend cut, 2-notch downgrade, sector underperformance + name residual return < −10%)

Disclosed limitations (per §1.1, §2.1)

  • 8 of 9 names pay dividends → American early-exercise risk on ITM puts (Merton Theorem 13). Pricing approximation: BS European-style for all wheel names; magnitude small at OTM entry, grows ITM near ex-dividend dates.
  • HAR-RV forecaster uses daily-frequency RV components, not 5-minute intraday (per BTZ §3.2.1 caveat).

Acceptance gates

  1. 9-name primary Sharpe ≥ 7-name strict − 0.05 (else strict becomes headline)
  2. Each ML/forecaster head meets criterion or falls back
  3. DSR ≥ 0.95
  4. PBO ≤ 0.30
  5. Reproducibility test passes
  6. Within-sector probe (KO/PEP correlation) reported regardless of pass/fail

Literature foundation (LITERATURE_FINDINGS.md)

8 papers read in full. Per-paper findings cataloged:

  • Bailey & López de Prado 2014, Deflated Sharpe Ratio + Eq. 9 N̂ correction
  • Bailey/Borwein/López de Prado/Zhu 2015, Probability of Backtest Overfitting via CSCV
  • Niculescu-Mizil & Caruana 2005, Platt-vs-isotonic calibration sample-size threshold (~200)
  • Hoeffding (1963) trader application, applied to live monitoring with 50/25/10% threshold semantics on the probability bound
  • Carr & Wu 2003/2004, Variance risk premia, evidence-base heterogeneity across instruments
  • Bollerslev/Tauchen/Zhou 2009, Quarterly forecast horizon empirically optimal; Hodrick SE for overlapping regressions
  • Black & Scholes 1973, BS empirical bias (overvalues high-var, undervalues low-var) is the source of VRP
  • Merton 1973, American early-exercise on dividend-paying stocks; rational pricing bounds
